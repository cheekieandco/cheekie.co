Package 01 — Cheekie Essentials

Day-of Coordination

Starting at $1,000

You've done the planning — we make sure it all comes together. Professional coordination for smaller events, without the cost of full-service planning. We step in during the final weeks to organize logistics, wrangle vendors, and keep the day running smoothly from first arrival to last load-out.

What's included

  • Two pre-event planning meetings — we review your plans, answer questions, and make sure every detail is covered before the big day.
  • Master event timeline — one streamlined schedule built from your details: photographer, hair & makeup, vendor arrivals, ceremony timing, and key moments. (You provide the vendor schedules; we make them make sense.)
  • Vendor confirmation — we contact each vendor beforehand to confirm arrival times, logistics, points of contact, and final details.
  • Rehearsal coordination (weddings) — we run your ceremony rehearsal so everyone knows exactly where to be and when.
  • Day-of coordination — from vendor arrivals through the close of your event we manage the timeline, field vendor questions, greet and direct guests, troubleshoot hiccups, and carry a stocked day-of emergency kit for whatever pops up.
  • Standard décor setup — placement of your décor: welcome table, guest book, card box, table numbers, centerpieces, and reserved signs.
  • Basic breakdown coordination — we oversee cleanup and gather your personal items before heading out.

Perfect for

  • Weddings & intimate receptions
  • Bridal & baby showers
  • Birthday celebrations
  • Engagement parties
  • Corporate gatherings & private events

The details

  • Flat rate: $1,000
  • Up to 75 guests
  • One coordinator
  • Within 15 miles of Alexandria, VA 22314 (travel available for an added fee)
  • Additional setup & décor services available

Package 02 — Cheekie Signature

One-Month Coordination

Starting at $2,000

You planned the celebration — we'll manage everything else. For hosts who want a pro to take the wheel during the final month, so the weeks leading up are something you actually enjoy. We finalize every moving piece, talk to all the vendors, and run the event from setup to send-off.

Everything in Essentials, plus

  • One-month planning kickoff — a comprehensive meeting about four weeks out where we take over logistics and begin final coordination.
  • Unlimited communication during the final month — questions, last-minute changes, the 11pm "wait, what about…" texts. We've got it.
  • Venue walkthrough — we walk the space with you (and vendors when needed) to finalize layout, load-in, and event flow.
  • Complete custom timeline — detailed and tailored for every vendor, family member, and key player.
  • Vendor management — we become the primary point of contact leading up to and throughout the event.
  • Full décor placement — ceremony & reception décor, welcome displays, seating charts, favors, specialty tables, signage, lounge, dessert displays, memory tables, candles, and personal details.
  • Rehearsal & rehearsal dinner coordination — we run the rehearsal and help with logistics at the dinner.
  • Event management — timeline execution, vendor coordination, family logistics, guest questions, ceremony cueing, reception flow, problem-solving, décor oversight, and a final walkthrough.
  • Vendor payments & gratuities — distributed according to your instructions.
  • End-of-night breakdown — we coordinate vendor load-out and make sure your personal items leave with the right people.

Starting investment

  • 25–49 guests from $2,000
  • 50–100 guests from $2,750
  • 100+ guests from $3,000

Pricing varies with guest count, venue logistics, décor complexity, number of vendors, event length, and staffing needs.
